# Focused sensitivity checks

These studies were narrower than the broader method experiments. Each responds to a specific doubt raised by a map, diagnostic or supervision discussion: whether Bus geography was an artefact, whether K=4 survived changes in seed and adjacent K, whether a different time cutoff altered the result, and whether the Rail solution remained credible across scope and K alternatives.

Their role is to qualify confidence rather than to manufacture certainty. A stable pattern is reported as more reproducible under the tested perturbation; an unstable or incomplete check remains visible as such. The final labels continue to come from the adopted pipeline, not from this directory.
